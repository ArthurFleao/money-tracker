# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Money Tracker to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: never share anyone's financial or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or any conduct that would reasonably be
  considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form (it is private) or by contacting a
maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
